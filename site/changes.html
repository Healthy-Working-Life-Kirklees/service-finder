What's changed

The Support Finder is a prototype. We're changing it often while coaches test it. This page lists the changes you might notice, newest first. Tell us what you think using the feedback links on the results.

5 November 2026

  • The dark blue bar of team links at the top of the main page has gone. The pages for the development team are now behind a single "Admin" link at the bottom of the main page, and need a team passcode. You don't need them to use the Support Finder.

4 November 2026

  • The tool has a new name: Healthy Working Life Support Finder. It used to be called the Service Finder.
  • We've taken the example questions off the start page. Describe the person's situation in your own words instead.
  • New: this "What's changed" page.
  • Click the logo at the top of any page to go back to the main page.
  • New: a "Give pilot feedback" button at the top of the main page. Use it as often as you like to tell us how the Support Finder worked for you. It takes about two minutes.

3 November 2026

  • "Suggest a service" in the top bar now has a fuller form. You can tell us about a service that's missing, who it's for, how to contact it and whether people can refer themselves.

2 November 2026

  • Who can use the Healthy Working Life grant-funded schemes is now clearer, to match the programme's requirements.
  • The printable directory is now on the site. Use "Printable directory" in the top bar. It has one service per page, indexes for North and South Kirklees, and a search box to jump to a service.
  • More services added to the list after the team checked them.

27 October 2026

  • Clearer wording about the prototype on the start page.
  • We now keep anonymous counts, such as how many searches are made and which links are opened. These don't identify anyone, and what you type is still not saved.

26 October 2026

  • "Get directions" and "Show on map" on service cards open Google Maps.
  • A new look for the start page, with the Healthy Working Life logo.

15 to 16 October 2026

  • Many more services added. The team checks each one before it appears in results.
  • New ways to give feedback: "Was this helpful?", "Report a problem with this listing", and telling us about a service we missed.
  • Works better with screen readers.
  • Fixed replies that said a follow-up question was coming when it wasn't.

14 October 2026

  • An "other options" button after the results, if you'd like to see different services.
  • The reply stays in view instead of jumping to the end of the cards.
  • Follow-up questions now always show when the AI asks one.

13 October 2026

  • Organisation logos on service cards.
  • Accessibility options in the top bar: bigger text, high contrast, and "Listen" to hear a service's details read aloud.

9 October 2026

  • The page can now be shown in Polish or Urdu, using the language menu in the top bar.

7 to 8 October 2026

  • First version of the prototype, in Healthy Working Life colours.

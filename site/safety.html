Worried about a child

Children's Services: Duty and Advice

For members of the public. 9am to 5pm, office hours.

01484 456848

dutyandadvice@kirklees.gov.uk

Outside 9am to 5pm: if a child is in immediate danger, call 999. Otherwise, call the relevant service, such as the NSPCC helpline below.

  • NSPCC helpline, for adults worried about a child: 0808 800 5000
  • If you are a child, call 01484 456848 or Childline on 0800 1111. Calls are free.
  • Professionals (24 hours): call Duty and Advice on 01484 414960. Then fill in the Duty and Advice contact form

Kirklees Healthy Working Life Service Finder

For the MVP team only. Every scheme and service the finder knows about, including those not yet on the live list. Only services on the live list are shown on the finder and given to the AI. Use this page to decide, for each service, whether to include it on the live list, give it further review, or exclude it completely. To change a service's details, use its "Edit on GitHub" link and open a pull request.

Record decisions

Enter the team passcode (the same one as the issues log) to record a decision on any service or edit its details. Without it you can still search and read everything.

Saved with each decision you make.

Decisions and edits are saved straight away and everyone on the team sees them. They reach the live finder only after they are applied to the data (on GitHub: Actions > Apply team changes > Run workflow, then merge the pull request it opens), so "waiting to be applied" means the team has made a change but the finder hasn't changed yet.

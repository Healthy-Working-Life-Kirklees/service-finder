Kirklees Healthy Working Life Service Finder

For the review team only. The first part shows anonymous usage counts. The rest is what people sent from the finder: "Was this helpful?", problems with a listing, and what's missing. None of it includes anyone's conversation. Contact details are removed from comments automatically, but a comment could still mention a name, so treat it as confidential and don't copy comments anywhere public. Feedback is a lead, not a fact: check with the service before changing a listing.

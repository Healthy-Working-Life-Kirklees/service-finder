What's available, where and when: for planning and commissioning

For the team and commissioners. Every service on the Support Finder's live list, with its venues, days and times, counted by ward. It comes from the same listings as the finder, so nothing is entered twice. Gaps are only as good as the data: many services haven't given their days, times or groups yet, and "nothing here" can mean "not told us yet". The "Data still to collect" view shows how much is missing.
